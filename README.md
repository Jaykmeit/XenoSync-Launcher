XenoSync Launcher is a Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 Launcher and Mod Manager that combines XV2 Revamp with additional mods to fully enjoy online multiplayer experience on just a few button touches. Easy setup, on just a few clicks and you're good to go.\
**Requires an official copy of Steam**, as you'll be needing to use Steam credentials for a Steam Depot download in case of downgrade requirement.
